## 2026-10-11

- **Defaults for the open questions in PLAN section 9** (the owner started Phase 0 without answering them, so the simplest option was taken; reversible): Python + Pillow + numpy as tool-only dependencies; `typescript-eslint` + `@eslint/js` as dev dependencies; balance sim to run through Vitest (no `tsx`); Vite `base` is `./` (works on any Pages path).
- **Deviation: numpy is used by the slicer.** Pillow alone is too slow on the 20 631 px tall enemies sheet. Tool-only, never shipped.
- **Deviation: labels are decoded, not read by eye.** The sheets hold ~11 000 sections (steve 1352, alex 1352, rana 1224, enemies 793, allies 460, animals 3345, villagers 609), far too many to catalogue visually. `tools/ocr_labels.py` matches the sheets' single bitmap font glyph by glyph (`tools/font_glyphs.json`, 36 glyphs) and `tools/build_catalog.py` derives ids and the catalogue from the decoded text.
- **Sheet structure (measured).** A section is a header strip plus a cell grid. The label box sits inside the strip (black text on cell-bg). Hero sheets have one header row per section, with the armor tier given by a title above each column group. Mob sheets have 1-2 header rows (name + animation labels); each animation owns the cell columns from its label to the next one. Cell separators are full-height/width 1 px lines; the label box interrupts the separator colour in the header rows, so vertical separators are measured below the headers.
- **Manifest entry format:** `{id, sheet, section, frames:[k...], fps, loop, tags, ship}`. `ship: true` entries are packed by `build_atlas.py`. Frames are `<sheet>.<section>.<k>` in `atlas.json`; flash silhouettes are `<key>@flash`. Hurt tint is not baked yet (mob sheets already carry red hurt frames).
- **Atlas v0:** all three heroes (idle, punch, swing, every armor and sword tier) = 1864 frames + flash variants, 1 page, ~600 KiB. Mobs are not shipped yet; the roster is confirmed at Checkpoint 0.
- **Font:** Pixelify Sans (OFL 1.1) bundled as WOFF2 in `public/fonts/` with `OFL.txt`; the direct GitHub download was blocked, so it was taken from the `@fontsource/pixelify-sans` npm package. Fonts are loaded before the first canvas draw.
- **Visual check:** Playwright (`playwright-core` + the preinstalled Chromium at `/opt/pw-browsers`) works headless in this environment; the hub screenshot shows Steve idle at x3, unsmoothed. `@playwright/cli` installed globally and its skill added under `.claude/skills/playwright-cli` (not committed).
- **`assets-work/` is scratch:** a safety check blocked a wildcard delete of it, so re-slicing overwrites files in place and old frame PNGs from earlier slicer iterations may remain. `sections.json` is the source of truth. The owner can delete `assets-work/` and re-run the tools at any time.
- **Fixed: base mobs missing from the catalogue.** Plain mobs (zombie, skeleton, creeper...) have a narrow name box that is not wide enough (>10% of the block width) to count as a header row, so it was ignored. `tools/build_catalog.py` now reads the strip above the first header row (or the stand-alone box just above the block) as the name, and treats rare animation labels (`ALWAYS_ANIM`) as animations. Result: base mobs, bosses and most of the brief's roster now have ids. Names and frame counts are auto-derived, not checked sprite by sprite; the contact sheets are there for that.
- **Manifest validator:** `tools/validate_manifest.py` checks fields, unique ids and that every listed frame exists.
- **Atlas v0 contents:** the three heroes plus the core mobs (zombie, skeleton, spider, creeper, slime big/medium/small) to prove the pipeline (~640 KiB). The rest is packed once the roster is confirmed.

## 2026-10-13 — v1.1 round (owner feedback)

- **Font: Tiny5 (OFL) through a 1-bit glyph atlas** replaces Pixelify Sans. Pixelify Sans is not pixel-aligned at any small size (22 OFL pixel fonts were measured in a headless browser: share of partially transparent pixels and fractional advances), which made canvas text blurry and unevenly weighted (its "2" even read as "8"). Tiny5 is fully crisp at its native 8 px, has the same line box as the old font (ascent 7, descent 2), is ~15% narrower, has lowercase, digits, punctuation and Latin-1 accents. All text is drawn from the atlas with whole-pixel advances and a hard 1 px shadow (`src/ui/font.ts`, `drawText`/`wrapText`/`fitText` in `src/ui/panel.ts`); damage numbers are cached per (string, colour). Runners-up: Fusion Pixel 10 px (most legible but 22% wider and 3 px taller), Silkscreen (no real lowercase), Press Start 2P (too wide).
- **Movement:** the renderer now interpolates between the last two 60 Hz steps (`Loop` passes `alpha`; entities keep `prevX/prevY` and `rx/ry`), the hero accelerates and brakes (full speed in ~0.1 s), the camera is integer-snapped around the rounded hero with a small look-ahead, the facing of attacks no longer fights the facing of movement, and heroes get a distance-driven walk cycle (legs shuffle +1/0/-1 px, body lifts on the passing step, dust and footstep sounds). The sheets contain no hero walk frames, hence the procedural cycle.
- **Hero abilities (Q / Shift):** data-driven registry in `src/abilities` (like weapons), HUD slot with cooldown, `Mods.abilityCd` / `abilityPower`. Steve: Iron Resolve, Alex: Arrow Rain, Rana: Frog Leap.
- **Input:** keyboard, gamepad (standard mapping) and touch (floating joystick plus ability/item/pause buttons) are merged by `MergedInput`.
- **Repository hygiene:** `CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/` are untracked and ignored (they stay in history; the local copy is the vault copy), `.env*`, keys and service-account files are ignored, `.env.example` added.

## 2026-10-13 — Accounts UI and settings (wave 3, agent A1)

- **Profile and Ranking are canvas screens; only text entry is DOM.** Profile (`src/ui/profileScreen.ts`) shows avatar, name, account badge (Guest / Local profile / Google / Email / Demo / Offline cloud), the sync line (`CloudSaveStore.status`), lifetime stats and the three best heroes. Avatar change is a canvas stepper over the owned heroes; sign in / create account use `app.openAccount`, and renaming uses the new `openNameEditor` in `authOverlay.ts` (cloud nickname with the live availability check, or the local profile name). Without Firebase it says "Cloud save disabled; playing locally." and still offers the local profile and the avatar. Pure text helpers live in `src/ui/accountInfo.ts` (tested).
- **Ranking** uses only what `LeaderboardView` supports: Today / This week / All time (online only) and a hero stepper (the list has 11 heroes, one tab each did not fit). Offline it is the player's own bests, one row per hero. Up to 50 rows are fetched, the window shows what fits (6 rows at 320x180) and scrolls with the wheel (new optional `Screen.onWheel`, wired in `main.ts`), keys, arrows or "Find me". Loading, error (with Retry) and empty states are drawn; stale answers are ignored.
- **`cameraLead` defaults to true** (the camera always led before the setting existed, so `false` would have changed the feel of every existing save). Migration keeps an explicit value; only saves without the field or with junk get the default. Tests in `tests/accessibility.test.ts` and `tests/save-v2.test.ts`.
- **Colour-blind mode is a cue remap, not a post-process filter** (`src/core/accessibility.ts`, `cb(color)`). Only the critical colour-coded cues are mapped: hostile telegraph, boss bar, low-HP vignette, level-up card borders and companion rarity colours. A filter over the whole canvas would also recolour the art the player has to recognise. Red-green modes turn "danger" violet (red melts into grass); one rarity ladder is used for all three modes. The palettes were chosen with a Machado (2009) simulation and the tests assert a minimum perceptual distance between rarities (>25 dE) and between danger, grass and the friendly telegraph. Hearts, XP and pickups were left alone: they differ by shape, not only by colour.
- **Reduce flashing** removes the lightning screen flash, the hit-flash silhouettes (enemies and hero), the 20 Hz invulnerability blink (steady 65% alpha instead), and the telegraph pulse, slows the creeper/TNT fuse blink from 8-12 Hz to 2 Hz, and turns screen shake off. The fuse cue stays (a slow blink) because it is gameplay information.
- **Text size 2x applies to the in-run HUD (timer, kill and emerald counters) and the DOM account dialogs; the other canvas UI stays 1x.** The menus are laid out for 320x180 with 9 px lines; doubling them would not fit without redesigning every screen, and the canvas is already zoomed 2-3x by `pickZoom`. Documented in the Settings hint line.
- **Touch controls:** 'auto' shows the controls on coarse pointers (`matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')`) or after the first touch, 'on' always, 'off' never (`TouchInput.setMode`, applied from `applySettings`).

## 2026-10-13 — Meta progression: missions, achievements, daily reward, results (wave 3, agent B)

- **Save: `stats.counters`** (open-ended `Record<string, number>`, sanitized like the other maps: safe keys only, counts clamped to MAX_COUNT, at most 480 keys at runtime) holds lifetime numbers that do not deserve a field (`kills.<enemy>`, `sec.<biome>`, `damageDealt`, `trades`, `best.level`, `wins.<hero>`, `missionsClaimed`, ...). `HeroStats` was left alone.
- **Missions are local-time and deterministic.** The daily set rolls from the local `yyyy-mm-dd` key and the weekly set from the ISO week key (Monday start), also keyed on which heroes the player owns so a class mission is never impossible. Rollover auto-claims finished missions of the expired set. Rerolls: 2 per local day, shared by daily and weekly. A saved day slightly in the future (clock moved back) is kept; one more than 30 days ahead is treated as stale.
- **Daily login reward:** 7-day cycle (50, 75, 100 + egg, 150, 200 + egg, 300 + 2 eggs, 400 + egg + rare egg); the streak keeps counting past day 7 while the reward loops. A missed local day restarts at day 1. Clock safety: a clock outside 2024..2100 is ignored, a clock behind the last claim never claims or rewinds the streak, and a stored claim more than 30 days in the future is discarded so one bad clock cannot lock the player out.
- **Progress sources:** per-run stats (`RunStats`, hooks in `RunState.onKill/update`, `damageEnemy`, `hurtPlayer`, trader and villager code, `useItem` in runScreen) are reduced to a `RunRecord` at run end (`finishRun`); eggs opened and upgrade ranks are picked up by `syncMeta`, which `main.ts` runs whenever the save's `lastModified` moves (so no other screen needed edits). Debug runs never touch the save or the leaderboard; quitting from the pause menu forfeits the run (as before).
- **Eggs opened** for achievements is `max(stats.eggsOpened, pullCount)` because the gacha only bumps `pullCount`.
- **Toasts:** one global queue (`ui/toast.ts`) drawn by `main.ts` top-right over every screen with the panel toast style.
- **Review helper:** `?screen=missions|achievements`, and `?screen=results&debug` (a made-up finished run that is saved but not sent to the leaderboard).

## 2026-10-13 — Balance regression hunt: mostly sampling noise, one bot artifact, one re-tune

- **The reported regression was mostly noise.** The v1.0 per-weapon table used 4 seeds per cell (`BAL_RUNS=8` halved), and the medians quoted as the v1.0 baseline (bow 9:27, crossbow 9:09, blaze rods 8:58, TNT 7:32, snowball 7:50, axe 7:04) are 4-seed medians; the newbie 3:44 came from the same small sample. Re-measured on the v1.0 commit with 24 seeds, the same bot gives bow 6:28, crossbow 5:59, blaze rods 6:16, lightning rod 6:30 (newbie 3:15 over 150 seeds), so v1.0 never reached the "8-10 minutes" this was compared with. One run's survival has a standard deviation of about 2 minutes; the old tables were good to about +-1:15.
- **Real v1.1 loss: about 0:40 on strong starters (mean 6:53 on v1.0 -> 6:15 on the head, 24-48 seeds).** Bisected (`git worktree` per commit plus environment switches in a throwaway tree) to the movement commit 4dc932d: acceleration (full speed in 0.1 s) against a bot that re-planned every 6 steps. Instant movement, doubled acceleration, or a bot that re-plans every 2 steps each win it back (+0:33 to +1:00). The spatial biome map, rosters, 35 new mobs, biome `danger` multipliers, the `slow` status, hero kits and abilities were switched off one by one and have no measurable effect (all within +-0:15). Table in `docs/BALANCE.md`.
- **Bot changes (documented, minimal):** the smart policy now re-plans every 2 steps (30 Hz) instead of 6, because the old rate was calibrated against instant velocity; the newbie keeps 10 Hz on purpose. The smart bot also steers away from lava (`lavaRepulsion`), which the v1.1 hot biomes made lethal for a bot that wanders in a straight line (3 of 12 blaze-rod runs ended in lava). The movement model was not touched: the loss is the bot's, not the player's.
- **Balance change: base regeneration 0.4 -> 0.7 HP/s** (`BASE_REGEN` in `data/upgrades.ts`, test in `tests/run.test.ts`). With the artifacts removed strong starters still died at 6-7 minutes, as in v1.0, so reaching the brief's "decent build reaches the Wither" needed a real re-tune. The wall is accumulated damage (30-60 HP per minute taken in minutes 3-5 against 24 HP per minute of regeneration), so regeneration is the lever that does not touch the BRIEF curves. Sweep on held-out seeds (7000+, 48 each, medians sword / bow / crossbow / blaze / lightning): 0.4 -> 5:26 / 6:24 / 7:25 / 5:56 / 6:41, 0.6 -> 4:59 / 7:34 / 7:42 / 6:25 / 8:11, 0.7 -> 4:58 / 8:53 / 8:42 / 7:23 / 9:17, 0.8 -> 5:43 / 9:32 / 8:53 / 9:13 / 10:04. 0.8 moves the sword-only median to the top of its band, so 0.7. Also screened and not taken: enemy damage slope 0.04/min (similar to regen 0.6 but changes a BRIEF curve), XP curve 8 + 5 x level (+0:30 for everyone), enemy HP slope 0.08 (nothing).

- **Residual risks:** (1) the re-tune is fitted to the bot: real players dodge better in some ways and worse in others, and use chests, companions, abilities and upgrades the bot ignores, so regeneration 0.7 may make human runs easier than the numbers suggest; companions and permanent upgrades stack on top of it. (2) Sword-only smart medians read 5:27-5:41 (v1.0 5:08 with its own bot, head 4:26 with the old bot), near the top of the 4-6 band. The rise is mostly the better bot (+0:40 at regeneration 0.4); regeneration moves the sword by less than 0:20 between 0.4 and 0.7, so lowering it would cost the strong starters about 1:00-1:15 per 0.1 for little gain; if sword runs feel too easy in play, look at early-game pressure instead. (3) Single-weapon cells still carry about +-0:30 of noise at 48 seeds; trust differences above one minute only. (4) The simulator test timeout was raised from 1 h to 4 h: a 48-seed full run takes about 100 minutes and the previous limit reported a spurious failure after writing the results.
